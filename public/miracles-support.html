Each person can make a set number of drawings a day. When you reach it, you can draw again the next day.

If a drawing can't be made from what you wrote, try saying it a different way.

Deleting your data

You can delete your book, its drawings and your account from inside the app. You can also email the address above with the subject “Delete my data” and we'll delete them for you.
