# Dolores Cannon — the time-travel class (Three Waves 1)

Illustrations for Sophie's short film of the passage in *The Three Waves of
Volunteers* (2:32:32–2:39:04): Laura, regressed, remembers being a young man in
a classroom on another world, learning to travel through portals — the rifts
are dangerous unless you go at the right time; go wrong and you're out of your
time slot and can never get back; "it's like crossing a river and you have to
go with the rapids."

The source map is `imageforge/docs/dolores-cannon-time.md` (Thread A). The
film's working audio is the banked v3 spine
(`dolores-time/clips/v3/V3-SPINE.mp3` in deckfactory Storage, ~46s, B1 → A);
the eight scenes here cover every sentence of the A cut plus the classroom
framing. Stills only — animation deliberately not started yet.

Recipe (the settled sage sandy mirror / Evan recipe, `docs/nde-watercolor.md`):
gpt-image-2 **edits**, `refs/sage-sandy-mirror.png` attached as a pure style
reference, **no written style description**, quality **medium**, 1024x1536.
Rendered 2026-08-16, ~$0.48 for the batch.

- `tt-*.webp` — the eight full-resolution renders (1024x1536, as returned by
  the model; webp is gpt-image-2's own output format here, not a re-encode).
- `prompts.json` — the exact prompt sent for every image (renderer-written).
- `timeclass.json` / `timeclass-labels.json` — the jobs + Assets-tab labels
  (also committed in imageforge `scripts/nde-watercolor/`).

Filed into the chat `time-travel-class-illustrations` Assets tab and the iOS
My Creations gallery; Storage copies at `dolores-time/art/v1/` (deckfactory).
